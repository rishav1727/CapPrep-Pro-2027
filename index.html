Key Grammar Rules

  • Subject-Verb Agreement: Singular subjects take verbs with -s ("The team plans").
  • Active vs Passive: "The developer fixed the bug" vs "The bug was fixed by the developer".
  • Gerunds after Prepositions: "Look forward to meeting you".

Correction Traps

  • Double Negatives: "I didn't see nobody" ❌ → "I saw nobody" ✅.
  • Parallel Structure: "She likes coding, reading, and swimming".
  • Pronoun Agreement: "Neither of the candidates has submitted".

⚙️ Bitwise Binary Mechanics
Left Shift (x << k): Multiplies x by 2^k → 6 << 1 = 12
Right Shift (x >> k): Divides x by 2^k → 12 >> 2 = 3
Bitwise XOR (a ^ b): 1 if bits differ → 5 ^ 3 = 6
Bitwise NOT (~x): Two's complement → ~5 = -(5+1) = -6
