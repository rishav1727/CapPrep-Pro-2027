GAME-BASED COGNITIVE ~20-30 Mins

🎮 Motion & Grid Challenge

Capgemini assesses spatial ability, inductive logic, and working memory through rapid interactive mini-games:

1. Motion Challenge (Pathfinding)
Move a ball from start to hole in minimum steps. Obstacles are movable blocks. Plan 3-4 moves ahead without random trial-and-error.
2. Grid Challenge (Spatial Working Memory)
Memorize dot sequences on 3x3/4x4 grids while solving quick symmetry calculations between screens. Tests simultaneous retention and processing.
3. Inductive & Deductive Reasoning
Identify rules governing 4 symbol matrices to predict the 5th. Focus on rotation degrees (45°/90°), color inversion, and element counts.

CORE CS FUNDAMENTALS Frequently Asked

⚡ Technical Rapid Fire

Must-know foundational questions frequently asked by Capgemini tech panels:

1. Process vs Thread & Deadlock Prevention
Process has its own address space; threads share parent process memory. Deadlock prevented by violating one of 4 Coffman conditions (e.g., resource ordering for Circular Wait).
2. 1NF to 3NF Normalization in simple terms
1NF = atomic values (no repeating groups). 2NF = 1NF + no partial dependency on composite PK. 3NF = 2NF + no transitive dependency (non-key depending on non-key).
3. Real-world example of Polymorphism
Payment Gateway Interface: `pay(amount)` implemented differently by `CreditCardPayment`, `UPIPayment`, and `NetBankingPayment`.

STAR METHOD BUILDER Behavioral QA

⭐ Master Behavioral Responses

Structure every behavioral answer using the proven STAR framework:

Q: "Tell me about a time you had a conflict with a teammate."
S (Situation): During our final year capstone project, our backend teammate disagreed on using REST vs GraphQL APIs.
T (Task): We needed to finalize our contracts within 48 hours to meet sprint deadlines.
A (Action): I organized a 30-minute sync, mapped payload bandwidth and caching requirements, and demonstrated REST fit our mobile client better.
R (Result): We delivered 2 days ahead of schedule, maintaining zero breaking schema changes.
